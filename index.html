What it does

Turns 12 everyday signals such as sleep, screen time and stress into one estimated mental health score.

Where ML fits

A regression model, trained offline on example data, learned how these signals relate to reported scores.

How answers are processed

Your answers are checked in the browser, sent to the prediction API, grouped by country, and scored by the model.

What the score means

A single number on the model's scale. It describes a statistical pattern, not your worth or your future.

Limitations

This is experimental. It cannot see your full context, shows correlation not causes, and is not a clinical diagnosis or a replacement for a mental health professional.
